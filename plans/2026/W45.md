## Groceries

### produce
- baby spinach
- broccoli
- cucumber
- lime: 1 tbsp (fresh juice)
- matchstick carrots
- minced garlic: 6 tsp (source says 4 extra-large cloves; rounded up from 1/2 tsp per clove, source says 2 cloves; 1/2 tsp per clove)
- romaine
- white onion: 1 (medium, diced; 1 1/2 cups)
- yellow onion: 2 (small, diced)

### meat
- chicken breast: 4 (boneless, skinless; 3-4)
- ground beef: 1 lb

### dairy
- cream cheese: 8 oz (Neufchatel (light), cubed and softened)
- egg: 6 (hard boiled)
- plain yogurt: 1 cup (ranch)
- shredded cheddar: 2 cup

### deli
- deli turkey: 1 lb (or deli ham)
- rotisserie chicken: 2.5 cup (shredded; leftover chicken works)

### pantry
- cannellini beans: 2 can (15 oz, drained and rinsed)
- chicken broth: 0.5 cup + 2 can (14.5 oz, low sodium)
- chicken gravy mix: 2 pkg (0.87 oz envelopes)
- cream of chicken soup: 1 can (10.5 oz, unsalted)
- diced green chiles: 1 can (7 oz)
- diced tomatoes: 1 can (15 oz, petite diced)
- egg noodles: 12 oz
- lemon juice: 0.5 tbsp (1/2-1 tbsp, start low)
- long grain rice (steamed, for serving)
- mayonnaise: 1 cup (ranch)
- nutritional yeast: 2 tbsp
- raw cashews: 0.5 cup (heaping; soak overnight without a high-powered blender)
- thin spaghetti: 1 box
- tomato sauce: 3 can (8 oz cans, 24 oz total)
- vegetable stock: 2 cup (low sodium; 1-2 cups, divided)

### frozen
- frozen corn: 1.25 cup (or fresh)
