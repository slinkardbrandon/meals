## Groceries

### Hy-Vee

#### produce
- apple (optional)
- baby spinach
- carrot: 19 oz
- cucumber
- fresh ginger: 4 tbsp (grated)
- lime: 1 tbsp (fresh juice)
- matchstick carrots (eyeball a bunch)
- minced garlic: 1 tsp + 2 tbsp (source says 2 cloves; 1/2 tsp per clove)
- mini cucumber (eyeball a bunch, chopped)
- potato: 38 oz
- romaine
- scallion (a handful, chopped)
- yellow onion: 4 (small, diced)

#### meat
- ground beef: 4 lb

#### deli
- deli turkey: 1 lb (or deli ham)
- rotisserie chicken: 2.5 cup (shredded; leftover chicken works)

### Target

#### dairy
- cream cheese: 6 tbsp + 8 oz (Neufchatel (light), cubed and softened)
- egg: 6 (hard boiled)
- plain yogurt: 1 cup (ranch)
- shredded cheddar
- sour cream: 10 tbsp (divided)

#### pantry
- Better Than Bouillon beef: 6 tsp
- cannellini beans: 2 can (15 oz, drained and rinsed)
- chicken broth: 2 can (14.5 oz, low sodium)
- diced green chiles: 1 can (7 oz)
- long grain rice: 2 cup (washed)
- mayonnaise: 1 cup (ranch)
- panko breadcrumbs: 0.75 cup
- rice vinegar: 8 tbsp (for the pickle, for the sauce)

#### frozen
- frozen corn: 1.25 cup (or fresh)
- peas: 13 oz
